Your data

Data Protection Act 2019

The Act gives you a copy of the personal data held about you, and the right to have it erased. Both are below. Neither is unlimited, and the limit is stated rather than left for you to discover.

Take a copy

A JSON file of your account, your role and clinic, the screenings you performed, the reviews you wrote and the actions recorded against you. Machine-readable, because a right to your data is not a right to a screenshot.

It does not contain the radiographs or the scores. A screening is a record of a patient held by the clinic, not yours because you uploaded it.

Download my data

Close this account

Removes your account and your ability to sign in, and unlinks every record from it.

It does not delete clinical records. The screenings and reviews stay with the clinic under the name already written into them, and the access trail keeps its rows. A log that somebody leaving could empty would answer nothing, and a patient's screening is not the operator's to erase.
